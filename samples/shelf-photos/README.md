## Try them

- **Tally:** sign in at [tally.qorelly.com](https://tally.qorelly.com), open **Stock-take**, choose a photo and run the stock-take agent or a quick scan. Nothing changes in your stock until you confirm.
- **Partner demo:** at [demo.tally.qorelly.com](https://demo.tally.qorelly.com), connect a business, then **Run stock-take** and pick a photo inside the embedded stock-take.

These copies are 1000 px on the long edge. The agent reads small labels better from a full-size phone photo, so expect a few unread or low-confidence lines; that's what the review step is for.
